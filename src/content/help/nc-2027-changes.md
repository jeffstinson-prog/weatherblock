North Carolina has a new homeowners policy program for new and renewal policies that take effect on or after June 1, 2027. The NC Rate Bureau announced it in circular P-25-3. Three parts of it affect roofs:

- **Roof payment schedule.** On HO 2, HO 3 and HO 5 policies, wind and hail damage to the roof surface is paid at a percentage of replacement cost, based on the roof's age and material. Roofs under 5 years old are paid at 100%. [What the schedule pays](/help/roof-payment-schedule/)
- **Cosmetic damage.** The program adds an exclusion for wind or hail damage that changes how a roof, siding, doors or windows look without stopping them from keeping weather out. You can buy that coverage back. [What counts as cosmetic](/help/cosmetic-damage/)
- **Matching.** The program adds an exclusion for matching undamaged material, with optional coverage you can buy back.

What to do: read your renewal, your declarations page and the endorsements on your policy. Ask your insurance agent which of these are on your policy and what it costs to change them.

If your wind and hail coverage comes from the Beach Plan (NCIUA), ask your agent how this applies to you. The Rate Bureau documents don't say.

We can't tell you what your policy will pay. Only your insurance company decides that.
