FORTIFIED is a roofing standard from the Insurance Institute for Business and Home Safety (IBHS), made to hold up better in high wind. A FORTIFIED Roof has:

- A sealed roof deck
- Ring-shank nails in a tighter nailing pattern
- Stronger edges: a wider drip edge and a fully adhered starter strip
- A roof covering that meets high wind ratings
- Wind and rain resistant attic vents

An independent FORTIFIED Evaluator documents the work. IBHS then issues a designation certificate. A first certificate lasts five years.

Insurance discounts for a FORTIFIED roof may be available in North Carolina's beach and coastal areas. Ask your insurance agent.
