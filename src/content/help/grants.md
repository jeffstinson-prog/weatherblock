There are, if your home is insured by the Beach Plan (NCIUA). Two NCIUA programs pay toward an IBHS FORTIFIED Roof:

- **Strengthen Your Roof:** up to $10,000, for homes between the Intracoastal Waterway and the ocean, like the island part of Ocean Isle Beach. [strengthenyourroof.com](https://strengthenyourroof.com/), 1-844-627-6637.
- **Strengthen Your Coastal Roof:** up to $6,000, for the rest of Brunswick County and other coastal areas. [strengthenyourcoastalroof.com](https://strengthenyourcoastalroof.com/), 1-877-472-6862.

How they work:

- You need an eligible NCIUA policy. The program sites check eligibility.
- Grants are first come, first served until the money runs out. NCIUA said in October 2025 that grants were still available. The sites were still taking applications on November 19, 2026, but don't say how much money is left. Call to check.
- After approval you have 18 months to finish the roof.
- A Certified FORTIFIED Roofing Contractor has to install the roof, and an independent FORTIFIED Evaluator documents the work.
- You pay the roofer. NCIUA pays the grant to you after it gets the FORTIFIED certificate, the roofer's final invoice and your W-9. The grant is currently treated as taxable income.
