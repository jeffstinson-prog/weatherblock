---
question: How do I change or cancel my inspection?
topic: Booking and inspections
order: 60
related: [how-to-book, be-home]
keywords: [reschedule, cancel, change, move, time, appointment]
---
Use the private link from your booking. It lets you pick a new time or cancel, with no password.

You can change it online until the window starts. After that, or if you can't find the link, call or text {{contact.phone}}.

Anyone with the link can change your booking, so keep it to yourself.
