No. {{insuranceClaims.youDo}} We don't file, handle or negotiate claims.

In North Carolina, someone you pay to negotiate or settle a property insurance claim for you has to be a licensed public adjuster, or a lawyer.

What we do: {{insuranceClaims.weDo}} You can give those to your insurance company.

The NC Department of Insurance advises that if you plan to file a claim, you make sure your insurance company can review the damage before repair work begins.
