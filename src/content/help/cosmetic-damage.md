It's part of the North Carolina homeowners program that starts with policies taking effect on or after June 1, 2027.

If the exclusion is on your policy, wind or hail damage that only changes how something looks isn't covered. The Rate Bureau's sample notice describes it as marring, pitting or other surface damage to a roof, siding, doors or windows that changes how they look but doesn't stop them from keeping out the weather as well as before.

The program also lets you buy that coverage back. Ask your insurance agent what it costs.

**Matching.** The program also adds an exclusion for matching. That means paying to replace undamaged material so it matches the repaired part, for example because the old color is no longer made. Optional matching coverage can be added to a policy.

We can't tell you what your policy will pay. Only your insurance company decides that.
