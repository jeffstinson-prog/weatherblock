question: Should I repair my roof or replace it?

A problem in one spot can often be repaired. Wear across the whole roof, repeated leaks, or damage to the wood under the shingles can make a new roof worth a look.

We base the advice on the inspection, not on the roof's age alone. You get it in writing.
