Near the ocean, wind and salt air do the most damage.

For wind, the FORTIFIED standard is a good checklist even without a grant: a sealed roof deck, ring-shank nails in a tighter pattern, a wider drip edge, a fully adhered starter strip, and a roof covering with high wind ratings.

For salt, metal parts like flashing, nails and vents corrode faster near salt water. Ask what they're made of.
