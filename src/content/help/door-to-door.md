Slow down. After storms, people go door to door offering free roof inspections. North Carolina's Insurance Commissioner, Mike Causey, warned in January 2026: "we have an epidemic of fraud, especially a roofing fraud."

The NC Department of Insurance says: "Always be suspicious of contractors who show up unsolicited, often after storms." If you think your roof is damaged, it says to contact your insurance company first, "or a roofer you choose."

Before you hire anyone, the Department advises:

- Get written estimates from two or three local companies.
- Ask for proof of license and insurance in writing.
- Call your insurance agent before you sign a contract or pay for repairs.
- Don't let a contractor work directly with your insurance company unless your agent approves.
- Don't pay in full up front, and don't sign a completion certificate until the work is done.
- Don't sign anything under pressure.
