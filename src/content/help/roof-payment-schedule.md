Starting with policies that take effect on or after June 1, 2027, North Carolina HO 2, HO 3 and HO 5 homeowners policies pay for wind and hail damage to the roof surface at a set percentage of replacement cost. The percentage depends on how old the roof is and what it's made of.

From the Rate Bureau's payment table:

- **Under 5 years old:** 100%, any material.
- **Asphalt or composition shingles:** 85% at 5 years, 72.5% at 10, 60% at 15, 47.5% at 20 and 35% at 25.
- **Metal, tile, slate, wood and other materials:** 90% at 5 years, 82.5% at 10, 75% at 15, 67.5% at 20 and 60% at 25.

The percentage includes labor, permits, tax, and overhead and profit.

Roof age is the year your policy period starts minus the year the roof was installed, as shown on your policy. Check that your policy has the right installation year.

The policy can be changed to pay replacement cost on the roof instead, by removing the roof payment schedule endorsement. For roofs 5 years and older that costs more. Ask your insurance agent.

We can't tell you what your policy will pay. Only your insurance company decides that.
