- Stay off the roof. Wet or damaged roofs are dangerous.
- If water is coming in, catch it, move what you can, and call us at {{contact.phone}}.
- Take photos of any damage you can see from the ground and inside the house.
- Book {{derived.anInspection}}. {{insuranceClaims.weDo}}
- If you plan to file a claim, check your policy for how soon you have to report damage, and make sure your insurance company can review the damage before repair work begins.
