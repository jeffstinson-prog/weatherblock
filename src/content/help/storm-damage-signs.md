---
question: How can I tell if a storm damaged my roof?
topic: Storms and leaks
order: 20
related: [after-storm, leak-now]
keywords: [hail, wind, missing shingles, granules, stains]
---
Things you might see:

- Missing, lifted, cracked or creased shingles
- Shingle granules (they look like coarse sand) in gutters or at the bottom of downspouts
- Dents in vents, flashing or gutters
- Water stains on ceilings, or wet spots in the attic
- Branches or debris on the roof

Some damage can't be seen from the ground. That's what the inspection is for.
