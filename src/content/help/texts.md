Only if you say yes on the booking form. We send:

- Your booking confirmation
- A reminder the evening before and the morning of
- A note when we're on the way
- A message if your time changes

Usually no more than {{texts.maxPerBooking}} per booking. No marketing texts.

Reply STOP to any text to stop them. We email your confirmation either way. [Text message terms](/text-messages/).
