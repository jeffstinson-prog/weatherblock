In North Carolina it's usually called a named storm deductible. It's a separate deductible for wind and hail damage from a storm with an official name: a hurricane, a tropical storm or a tropical depression.

It's usually a percentage of your dwelling coverage (Coverage A), not a flat amount. The NC Department of Insurance gives this example: "A 2% named storm deductible on a home insured for $300,000, would be $6,000."

It starts when the National Weather Service issues a watch, warning or advisory for a named storm anywhere in North Carolina. It ends 24 hours after the last watch or warning ends, or after the last advisory, whichever is later.

Some policies also have a percentage deductible for any wind or hail damage, named storm or not.

Your deductibles are on your policy's declarations page. Your insurance agent can tell you which ones you have.
